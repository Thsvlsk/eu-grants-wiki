Can you charge personnel costs after the project ends?

Employees (A.1): all programmes except SMP ESS and CUST/FISCLast reviewed 2026-10-15

In short

In the AGA, actual costs must be incurred in the period set out in Article 4, the action duration. The exception is the cost of drafting and submitting the final report, which is eligible even if incurred after the action duration; it may also include the cost of personnel necessary for a project review or to prepare the final report AGA v2.0, pp. 40 and 43.

The short answer

Yes, but only for drafting and submitting the final report, including a project review before it, and not for any other action activities p. 43. You use the daily rate of the last reporting period and add the extra days to that period, with no separate daily rate p. 54. The maximum declarable day-equivalents does NOT apply to those additional days p. 55.

How it works

  1. Check what the work is. Drafting and submitting the final report, or participating in a project review carried out by the granting authority before the submission of the final report p. 43.
  2. Take the daily rate of the last reporting period. There is NO need to calculate a separate daily rate p. 54.
  3. Add the extra days to the days declared in the final reporting period p. 54.
  4. Do not apply the maximum declarable day-equivalents to these additional days p. 55.

The AGA gives no worked example with numbers for this case.

Watch out

Only the reporting work, nothing else.

However, they can NOT include any other action activities foreseen in the Annex 1 and undertaken after the end date of the action.

— AGA v2.0, Art. 6.1, p. 43

Not only personnel. The costs for reporting at the end of the action also include the cost of certificates on the financial statements (CFS) required by the Grant Agreement p. 43.

New in version 2.0. The AGA lists, among the changes of version 2.0, a clarification of how to calculate the daily rate for eligible personnel costs incurred after the end of the action p. 4.

Depends on your programme

Who this article covers. Employees under Article 6.2.A.1, in:

all programmes except SMP ESS, CUST/FISC or where declared ineligible in the call conditions

— AGA v2.0, Art. 6.2.A.1, p. 50

Articles 6.1 and 6.2.A.1 give no programme-specific variant of this rule.
