About this wiki

This wiki explains the rules of EU grants in plain English, for the people who have to apply them: project managers, finance officers and administrators.

How it works

Each answer starts with the short version, then explains how the rule works, what to watch out for and where the rule depends on your programme. Every rule points to the page of the official document it comes from, with the version that was checked. An article is only published after it has been checked against its source.

What it is not

Not an official EU website. An independent project, not affiliated with the European Commission. The documents linked here are the reference: always check your own Grant Agreement.
